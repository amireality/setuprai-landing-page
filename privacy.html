PRIVACY POLICY

EFFECTIVE DATE: JULY 27, 2026

01. INFORMATION WE COLLECT

When you use the SetuprAI Chrome Extension, we temporarily process the text you type into third-party AI chat interfaces (such as ChatGPT, Claude, etc.) solely for the purpose of re-engineering and optimizing your prompt.

  • No Persistent Storage of Prompts: We do not log, save, or persistently store your prompts or the AI's responses on our servers. The text is processed in-memory via our API and immediately discarded.
  • Account Information: We collect your email address and authentication details provided by Google Identity or Supabase to manage your subscription tier and credit limits.

02. THIRD-PARTY SERVICES

SetuprAI utilizes external APIs to function:

  • Payment Processing: We use Razorpay to process subscriptions. We do not store your credit card information.
  • AI Processing: The prompt engineering is powered by third-party LLMs (such as OpenAI/Anthropic APIs). Your data is passed securely to these APIs exclusively for prompt enhancement.

03. DATA SECURITY

We implement industry-standard encryption to protect your account data during transit and at rest. Since we do not store your chat prompts, the most sensitive data remains securely on your local device or with the primary chat provider you are using.

04. CONTACT

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at support@setupr.com.
